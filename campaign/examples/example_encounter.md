# Example Encounter — “The Shelf Mimic Showdown”

## 1. Encounter Identity
**Name:** The Shelf Mimic Showdown  
**Location:** Floor 01 — Commerce District  
**Theme:** Consumer horror  
**Broadcast Hook:** “Will they find a bargain or a bite?”  
**Sponsor Involvement:** SHOP ’N’ SLOP™

---

## 2. Mobs

### Shelf Mimic (x3)
**Stats:** Medium mimic  
**Gimmick:** Two loot rolls — one real, one mimic  
**Weakness:** Alphabetizing items stuns them  
**Behavior:** Pretends to hold useful items  

### Sponsor Drone (x1)
**Stats:** Light drone  
**Gimmick:** Drops random loot or hazards  
**Weakness:** Emotional overload  
**Behavior:** Records the fight  

---

## 3. Environment
**Hazards:**  
- Aisle loops  
- Price scanner malfunctions  
- Freezers fog with faces  

**Moving Parts:**  
- Conveyor floor sections  
- Shifting shelves  

**Traps:**  
- Falling product displays  

**Glitches:**  
- Wrong items appear  
- Aisle numbers repeat  

**Audience Triggers:**  
- “Vote: Should the shelves attack?”  

---

## 4. Cinematic Beats
**Intro narration:**  
The aisle lights flicker. A shelf shudders. Something inside growls.

**Announcer A:**  
“Contestants have entered a high-risk shopping environment.”

**Announcer B:**  
“EVERYTHING IS ON SALE! INCLUDING THEIR LIVES!”

**Mid-fight twist:**  
A freezer door opens and screams.

**Kill cam moment:**  
A mimic explodes into coupons.

**Loot reveal:**  
One real item, one cursed item.

**Sponsor ad break:**  
“SHOP ’N’ SLOP™ — Where savings bite back!”

---

## 5. Rewards
**XP:** Standard encounter  
**Loot:**  
- Shelf Mimic Snack Pack  
- Sponsor coupons  
- Random item from the mimic  

**Reputation:**  
+1 Audience Interest  
**Pet treats:**

**Sponsor coupons:**
