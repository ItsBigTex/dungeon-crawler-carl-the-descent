# Floor 01 Districts — Brownwood, Texas

## 1. The Neighborhood (Brownwood Residential)
The crawlers emerge in a near‑perfect recreation of Brownwood’s quiet neighborhoods:
- Familiar one‑story homes  
- Oak trees lining the streets  
- Pickup trucks in driveways  
- Porch lights glowing  

But something is wrong:
- The street signs are spelled slightly incorrectly  
- The houses have too many windows  
- The sky is too bright for being underground  
- The moon hangs low and unmoving  

### Hazards
- Wrong shadows  
- Houses rearranging internally  
- Lawnmower Goblins patrolling yards  
- Mailboxes that whisper  

### Broadcast Hook
“Contestants return to Brownwood! Will nostalgia save them or destroy them?”

---

## 2. The Commerce District (HEB + Walmart + Gas Stations)
A surreal mash‑up of Brownwood’s commercial hotspots:
- A distorted HEB  
- A too‑wide Walmart  
- A gas station with 30 pumps  
- A Sonic that loops back into itself  

Every aisle, pump, and freezer is an encounter.

### Hazards
- Shelf Mimics  
- Glitched price scanners  
- Aisles that repeat  
- Freezers that fog over with faces  

### Broadcast Hook
“Welcome to SHOP ’N’ SLOP™ Brownwood Edition!”

---

## 3. Brownwood High School
A near‑perfect recreation of Brownwood High School:
- Hallways too long  
- Classrooms too clean  
- Trophy cases filled with unfamiliar names  
- The intercom still works  

### Hazards
- Hallway stretching  
- Locker mimics  
- Repeating announcements  
- Classroom illusions  

### NPC
**Ms. Bell** — trapped in the tutorial loop.

### Broadcast Hook
“Education is important! Survival is mandatory!”

---

## 4. Downtown Strip (Baker Street, Center Ave, Depot District)
A warped version of Brownwood’s downtown:
- Baker Street curves in a perfect circle  
- The Lyric Theatre marquee flickers random phrases  
- The Depot District has too many alleys  
- The courthouse clock counts backward  

### Hazards
- Audience-triggered events  
- Sponsor drones  
- Glitched storefronts  
- Forced mini‑games  

### Broadcast Hook
“Billions of viewers are watching Brownwood’s finest!”

---

## 5. Gordon Wood Stadium (Boss Arena)
A massive underground recreation of Gordon Wood Stadium:
- Thousands of empty seats  
- A colossal jumbotron  
- Spotlights that track crawlers  
- The field is too green  

### Hazards
- Memory echoes  
- Sound distortion  
- Audience profile manipulation  

### Broadcast Hook
“THE FIRST EVENT BEGINS!”
