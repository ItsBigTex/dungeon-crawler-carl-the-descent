# Floor 01 — THE HOMECOMING (Brownwood, Texas)

A perfect replica of Brownwood, Texas — recreated underground with uncanny precision and subtle wrongness. This is the tutorial floor, designed to unsettle crawlers by presenting their hometown exactly as they remember it… except everything is slightly off.

## Floor Tagline
**“Welcome to Brownwood. Population: You.”**

## Theme
Small‑town familiarity → creeping dread  
Texas charm → manufactured illusion  
Nostalgia → weaponized

## Purpose
This floor teaches:
- The dungeon can replicate entire towns  
- The AI manipulates familiarity  
- The audience watches everything  
- Secrets matter  
- Pets matter  
- Reality is manufactured and unstable  

## Districts
1. The Neighborhood (Brownwood residential streets)  
2. The Commerce District (HEB, Walmart, gas stations, local shops)  
3. Brownwood High School  
4. The Downtown Strip (Baker Street, Center Ave, Depot District)  
5. Gordon Wood Stadium (Boss Arena)

## Boss
**THE HOMETOWN HERO**  
A memory‑constructed humanoid built from fragments of each crawler’s past in Brownwood.

## Key NPC
**Ms. Bell** — a former crawler trapped in the tutorial loop.

## Special Mechanic
**SHOW PROFILES** — each crawler receives an audience‑assigned identity.

## Reward
A loot box containing a **key** that should not exist.
