# Boss — THE FLOOR MANAGER

## Identity
A massive corporate-looking creature overseeing Floor 02’s machinery.  
It wears:
- A suit  
- A tie  
- A dozen ID badges  
- A smile that never changes  
- A clipboard with infinite pages  

## Personality
- Cheerful  
- Corporate  
- Ruthless  
- Bureaucratic  
- Terrifying  

## Cinematic Intro
The machinery stops.  
Lights flicker.  
A spotlight hits the arena.

The Floor Manager steps out, clapping.

“WELCOME, TEAM!  
IT’S TIME FOR YOUR PERFORMANCE REVIEW!”

---

## Phase 1 — Evaluation
The Manager analyzes each crawler:

- “HAROLD: EXCEEDS EXPECTATIONS.”  
- “MARVIN: NEEDS IMPROVEMENT.”  
- “PHILIP: MEETS EXPECTATIONS.”  
- “JAROD: UNPROFESSIONAL HUMOR DETECTED.”  
- “MIKE: EMOTIONAL MISREPRESENTATION FLAGGED.”  
- “BRAD: FILE NOT FOUND.”  

**Mechanic:**  
Targets one crawler per round with **PERFORMANCE REVIEW**.

---

## Phase 2 — Team Building
The Manager forces cooperation.

**MANDATORY TEAM BUILDING**  
All crawlers must perform a coordinated action or suffer penalties.

Examples:
- Everyone must move at least 10 feet  
- Everyone must attack the same target  
- Everyone must use a bonus action  

Failure triggers:
**TEAM FAILURE — PRODUCTIVITY LOSS**

---

## Phase 3 — Restructuring
The battlefield rearranges.

**RESTRUCTURING**  
Walls move.  
Conveyors activate.  
Hazards spawn.  
Gravity shifts.

The Manager says:
“CHANGE IS GOOD FOR PRODUCTIVITY!”

---

## Phase 4 — Termination
The Manager attempts to kill a crawler.

**TERMINATION**  
A devastating attack targeting the lowest‑performing crawler.

---

## Defeat
When the Manager falls, it doesn’t die immediately.

It sees the key.

It freezes.

It whispers:
“Oh.”

Then:
“You’re those crawlers.”

And kills itself.

The stairwell opens.

The AI announces:
“CONGRATULATIONS! YOU HAVE COMPLETED THE TUTORIAL.”

Then:
“FLOOR 3 HAS BEEN LOCKED.  
PLEASE WAIT FOR ADMINISTRATOR APPROVAL.”
