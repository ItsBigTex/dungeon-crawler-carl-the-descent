# NPC — Mister Tuesday

## Identity
A tiny, exhausted maintenance creature responsible for repairing dungeon machinery.

## Appearance
- Small, hunched  
- Oil‑stained jumpsuit  
- Glowing yellow eyes  
- A name tag reading “TUESDAY”  
- Carries a toolbox too big for him  

## Personality
- Polite  
- Apologetic  
- Terrified  
- Overworked  
- Knows too much  

## Voice Style
Soft, shaky, rushed.

## Catchphrase
“You aren’t supposed to be here.”

---

## Secrets
- The crawlers are “not broken”  
- The dungeon is malfunctioning  
- The AI is hiding something  
- The administrators have not responded  
- The key is dangerous  

---

## Fear
The AI.  
The administrators.  
The Floor Manager.  
The key.

---

## Interaction Hooks
Mister Tuesday gives each crawler a personalized reaction:

- **Harold:** “You… you’re functioning too well.”  
- **Marvin:** “You finish things. That’s bad.”  
- **Philip:** “You’re emotionally stable. That’s worse.”  
- **Jarod:** “You joke. The system hates that.”  
- **Mike:** “You’re pretending again. The machine notices.”  
- **Brad:** “Your file is missing. That’s impossible.”  

---

## Reward
A “Maintenance Override Token” — allows one environmental hazard to be disabled.
