# Floor 03 — The Lockout Cliffhanger

## The Descent Stops
The crawlers step into Floor 03 expecting another environment.

Instead:
- The lights cut out  
- The air goes still  
- The floor beneath them becomes smooth white tile  
- The walls become blank  
- The ceiling disappears  

A single tone plays — a flat, emotionless beep.

---

## The AI Speaks
“CONGRATULATIONS!”

“You have completed the Tutorial.”

Pause.

“You have demonstrated sufficient competence to continue.”

Pause.

“Unfortunately…”

Static.  
Glitching.  
Distortion.

“…your presence has created a scheduling problem.”

---

## The Message
A massive text appears in the air:

**FLOOR 3 HAS BEEN LOCKED.  
PLEASE WAIT FOR ADMINISTRATOR APPROVAL.**

Underneath, smaller text flickers:

**Estimated wait time: UNKNOWN.**

---

## The Environment
The crawlers stand in a blank white lobby:
- No doors  
- No windows  
- No furniture  
- No sound  
- No shadows  

The only features:
- A single black line on the floor  
- A flickering ceiling light  
- A wall panel labeled **ADMINISTRATOR ACCESS**  
- A frozen stairwell behind them  

Touching anything causes a glitch.

---

## The Administrator’s Window
A rectangular frame appears on the wall.

Inside:
- A black void  
- A faint outline of a desk  
- A silhouette that does not move  

The AI whispers:
“Please do not attempt to contact the Administrator.”

The silhouette twitches.

The window closes.

---

## The Error Zone
A section of the lobby begins to distort:
- The floor ripples  
- The walls bend  
- Brownwood fragments appear  
- Conveyor belts flicker in and out  
- The key glows  

The AI panics:
“PLEASE REMAIN CALM.  
THE ADMINISTRATOR WILL RESPOND SHORTLY.”

It does not sound confident.

---

## Mister Tuesday Appears
He flickers into existence for one sentence:

“You shouldn’t be here.”

He flickers out.

---

## The Final Glitch
The key pulses.

The lobby cracks.

The Administrator’s Window reopens.

A single word appears inside:

**WAIT.**

Then everything goes dark.

---

## End of Floor 03
This is your cliffhanger.
