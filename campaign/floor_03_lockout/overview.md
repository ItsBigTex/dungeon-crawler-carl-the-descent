# Floor 03 — LOCKOUT

## Floor Tagline
**“Please wait. Your existence is important to us.”**

## Theme
Administrative cosmic horror  
Suspended reality  
System failure  
Isolation  
Waiting for something that should not exist

## Purpose
Floor 03 is not a floor.  
It is a **halt state** — a forced pause in the dungeon’s processing pipeline.

This floor teaches:
- The dungeon is not fully autonomous  
- The AI is terrified of something above it  
- The administrators are real  
- The key is a breach  
- The crawlers’ presence is a problem  
- The system is failing  

## What the Crawlers Experience
When they descend from Floor 2:
- The lights go out  
- The machinery stops  
- The air becomes still  
- The AI glitches  
- The stairwell seals behind them  
- The next floor does not load  

A single message appears:

**FLOOR 3 HAS BEEN LOCKED.  
PLEASE WAIT FOR ADMINISTRATOR APPROVAL.**

Then nothing.

## The Space Itself
Floor 03 is:
- A blank white corridor  
- A featureless lobby  
- A waiting room with no doors  
- A place with no sound  
- A place with no time  
- A place that should not exist  

The crawlers are not meant to be here.

## Districts
Floor 03 has no districts.  
Only zones of increasing wrongness:

1. **The White Lobby**  
2. **The Frozen Stairwell**  
3. **The Administrator’s Window**  
4. **The Error Zone**

## Boss
There is no boss.  
There is only **the absence of one**.

## NPC
There are no NPCs.  
Except sometimes Mister Tuesday appears for one sentence and vanishes.

## Special Mechanic
**SYSTEM SUSPENSION** — actions feel delayed, repeated, or ignored.

## Reward
None.  
Floor 03 is a narrative pivot, not a reward floor.

## Exit
There is no exit.  
Not yet.
