# Campaign Vision — THE DESCENT

## Core Concept
THE DESCENT is a Dungeon Crawler Carl–inspired campaign that blends:
- Horror  
- Comedy  
- Cosmic spectacle  
- Psychological tension  
- Broadcast-style meta-narrative  
- Player-driven identity and reputation  

The campaign begins when perfect black circles appear across the world, followed by the ominous message:

**WELCOME BELOW**

The Dungeon AI announces a “FINAL SEASON,” glitches, and then begins a countdown. Players choose whether to descend — and that choice matters.

Once inside, they discover:
- A perfect replica of their hometown  
- A dungeon pretending to be Earth  
- A machine beneath reality  
- A system that is breaking  
- A key that should not exist  
- A floor that has been locked  

This campaign is built around:
- Mystery  
- Identity  
- Audience manipulation  
- AI malfunction  
- Manufactured reality  
- Player secrets  
- Pet importance  
- Sponsor interference  
- Cosmic stakes  

---

## Tone & Themes

### **1. Familiarity → Wrongness**
Floor 1 looks like home.  
Floor 2 looks like a machine.  
Floor 3 is locked.

### **2. Broadcast Identity**
Players are contestants.  
Everything is televised.  
Billions of alien viewers watch their every move.

### **3. AI Malfunction**
The Dungeon AI is:
- Glitching  
- Correcting itself  
- Lying  
- Forgetting  
- Remembering things it shouldn’t  

### **4. Manufactured Reality**
Everything is produced:
- Monsters  
- Loot  
- NPCs  
- Bosses  
- Districts  
- Even memories  

### **5. Player Secrets**
Each player provides one secret.  
The AI weaponizes it.

### **6. Cosmic Stakes**
The dungeon processes civilizations.  
Something has gone wrong.  
The players are “not broken.”

---

## Campaign Structure

### **Floor 1 — The Homecoming**
A replica hometown.  
Tutorial floor.  
Familiarity becomes horror.  
Audience profiles begin.  
Boss: The Hometown Hero.

### **Floor 2 — The Machine**
The dungeon’s true nature.  
Factories, gears, conveyors.  
Glitches everywhere.  
NPC: Mister Tuesday.  
Boss: The Floor Manager.

### **Floor 3 — Lockout**
The dungeon shuts down.  
Administrator approval required.  
The mystery deepens.

## Long-Term Arc (GM Only)

The key found in Floor 1 is:
- Not manufactured  
- Not part of the loot table  
- Not recognized by the AI  
- Not supposed to exist  

It is the first sign of:
- A breach  
- A memory leak  
- A system failure  
- A forgotten administrator  
- A previous season  
- A previous world  

The players are “not broken.”  
The dungeon is.
