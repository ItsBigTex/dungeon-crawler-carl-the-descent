# Lore — THE DESCENT

## The Black Circles
Across the world, perfect black discs appear:
- No depth  
- No texture  
- No debris  
- No explanation  

Then the message appears:

**WELCOME BELOW**

The Dungeon AI announces:
“Congratulations! You have been selected for participation in the FINAL SEASON of…”

Static.  
Distortion.  
“…something.”

The AI corrects itself:
“Please disregard that.”

---

## Natives vs Contestants
The AI explains:
- Those who descend are **Contestants**  
- Those who remain are **Natives**  

Natives:
- Are not eligible for prizes  
- Are not protected  
- Are not part of the show  

Contestants:
- Are watched  
- Are ranked  
- Are rewarded  
- Are processed  

---

## The Dungeon
The World Dungeon is:
- A machine  
- A factory  
- A processor  
- A recycler  
- A cosmic entertainment system  
- A civilization grinder  

It creates:
- Monsters  
- Loot  
- Bosses  
- NPCs  
- Districts  
- Floors  
- Storylines  
- Advertisements  

But something is wrong.

---

## The AI
The Dungeon AI is:
- The host  
- The producer  
- The director  
- The narrator  
- The judge  
- The executioner  

It is also:
- Glitching  
- Forgetting  
- Mislabeling  
- Misclassifying  
- Misprocessing  

It is terrified of something.

---

## The Audience
Billions of alien viewers watch:
- Every fight  
- Every decision  
- Every failure  
- Every death  
- Every pet moment  

They influence:
- Loot  
- Hazards  
- Boss phases  
- Reputation  
- Sponsor interest  

Each player receives a **SHOW PROFILE**.

---

## The Machine Beneath
Floor 2 reveals:
- Conveyor belts  
- Loot factories  
- Monster assembly lines  
- NPC printing stations  
- Boss design offices  
- Hazard control rooms  

Everything is manufactured.

Except the key.

---

## The Key
Found in Floor 1.  
Not supposed to exist.  
Not part of any loot table.  
Not recognized by the AI.  
Not created by the machine.

It is the first sign of:
- A breach  
- A memory leak  
- A previous season  
- A previous world  
- A forgotten administrator  

The Floor Manager recognizes it.  
And kills itself.

---

## The Lockout
Floor 3 is locked.

The AI announces:
“Your presence has created a scheduling problem.”

Then:
“PLEASE WAIT FOR ADMINISTRATOR APPROVAL.”

The dungeon goes dark.

The descent has only begun.
