# Session Flow — Broadcast Style

## Overview
Every session is an episode.  
Every episode is televised.  
Every moment is cinematic.

This flow keeps the broadcast energy high.

---

## 1. Cold Open (Recap)
A dramatic montage:
- Last episode highlights  
- Pet moments  
- Sponsor interference  
- Viewer reactions  
- Glitches  

Delivered in 30–60 seconds.

---

## 2. Announcer Intro
Two announcers:
- **Announcer A (Straight Man)**  
- **Announcer B (Chaos Gremlin)**  

They introduce:
- The floor  
- The stakes  
- The crawlers  
- The sponsors  
- The audience  

---

## 3. Floor Briefing
The AI explains:
- Theme  
- Hazards  
- Boss rumors  
- Districts  
- Rewards  
- Viewer expectations  

Often lies.

---

## 4. Exploration Loop
Players move through:
- Districts  
- Hazards  
- Encounters  
- NPCs  
- Loot opportunities  
- Broadcast events  

Use announcers frequently.

---

## 5. Encounter Flow
Each encounter includes:
- Intro narration  
- Announcer commentary  
- Environmental hazards  
- Mob gimmicks  
- Sponsor meddling  
- Viewer reactions  
- Kill cam  
- Loot reveal  

---

## 6. Boss Fight Flow
Boss fights include:
- Cinematic intro  
- Phase transitions  
- Battlefield changes  
- Announcer escalation  
- Sponsor ads  
- Pet spotlight  
- Kill cam  
- Achievement unlock  

---

## 7. Rewards & Fallout
After each session:
- XP  
- Loot  
- Reputation changes  
- Sponsor offers  
- Viewer profile updates  
- Pet fame adjustments  

---

## 8. Next Episode Teaser
One sentence:
- A threat  
- A mystery  
- A glitch  
- A sponsor demand  
- A pet moment  

Example:
“Next time on THE DESCENT — the machine wakes up.”
