# Floor Workflow — THE DESCENT

## Overview
Floors are the backbone of the campaign.  
Each floor has:
- A theme  
- A structure  
- Districts  
- Hazards  
- Mobs  
- Boss  
- NPCs  
- Broadcast layer  
- Loot economy  
- Chaos tables  

This workflow helps you build new floors quickly.

---

## Step 1 — Choose a Theme
Themes should be:
- Visually distinct  
- Mechanically unique  
- Emotionally impactful  
- Broadcast-friendly  

Examples:
- The Homecoming  
- The Machine  
- The Lockout  
- The Carnival  
- The Flood  
- The Silence  
- The Pet Floor  
- The Corporate Floor  

---

## Step 2 — Define Districts
Each floor has 3–6 districts:
- Neighborhood  
- Convenience District  
- School  
- Entertainment District  
- Stadium  

Each district should have:
- A purpose  
- A vibe  
- A hazard  
- A mob type  
- A loot type  
- A broadcast hook  

---

## Step 3 — Build Hazards
Hazards include:
- Environmental  
- Psychological  
- Mechanical  
- Social  
- Broadcast  

Examples:
- Gravity shifts  
- Wrong clocks  
- Glitching doors  
- Sponsor drones  
- Audience-triggered events  

---

## Step 4 — Create Signature Mobs
Each mob should have:
- A gimmick  
- A weakness  
- A personality  
- A broadcast identity  
- A loot drop  

Examples:
- Lawnmower Goblins  
- Shelf Mimics  
- Hallway Stretchers  
- Conveyor Gremlins  

---

## Step 5 — Build the Boss
Bosses should be:
- Personal  
- Thematic  
- Cinematic  
- Multi-phase  
- Broadcast-heavy  

Examples:
- The Hometown Hero  
- The Floor Manager  

---

## Step 6 — Add NPCs
NPCs should:
- Teach something  
- Warn something  
- Hide something  
- Fear something  

Examples:
- Ms. Bell  
- Mister Tuesday  

---

## Step 7 — Build the Broadcast Layer
Include:
- Announcer lines  
- Sponsor ads  
- Viewer polls  
- Highlight triggers  
- Reputation rewards  

---

## Step 8 — Build Loot & Economy
Include:
- Shops  
- Vendors  
- Loot tables  
- Sponsor kiosks  
- Pet gear  

---

## Step 9 — Add Chaos Tables
Chaos tables include:
- Viewer votes  
- Sponsor interference  
- Trap malfunctions  
- Pet chaos  
- Glitches  

---

## Step 10 — Write the Floor Packet
Each floor packet includes:
- Overview  
- Districts  
- Hazards  
- Mobs  
- Boss  
- NPCs  
- Loot  
- Chaos  
- Broadcast  
- Map notes
