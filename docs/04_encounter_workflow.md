# Encounter Workflow — THE DESCENT

## Overview
Encounters are cinematic, chaotic, and broadcast-driven.

This workflow helps you build encounters quickly.

---

## Step 1 — Encounter Identity
Define:
- Name  
- Location  
- Theme  
- Broadcast hook  
- Sponsor involvement  

---

## Step 2 — Mobs
Each mob needs:
- Stats  
- Gimmick  
- Weakness  
- Personality  
- Loot drop  
- Pet interaction  

---

## Step 3 — Environment
Include:
- Hazards  
- Moving parts  
- Traps  
- Glitches  
- Audience triggers  

---

## Step 4 — Cinematic Beats
Include:
- Intro narration  
- Announcer A line  
- Announcer B line  
- Mid-fight twist  
- Phase shift (if applicable)  
- Kill cam  
- Loot reveal  
- Sponsor ad break  

---

## Step 5 — Rewards
Include:
- XP  
- Loot  
- Reputation  
- Pet treats  
- Sponsor coupons
