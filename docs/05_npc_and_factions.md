# NPCs & Factions — THE DESCENT

## NPC Design
NPCs should:
- Teach  
- Warn  
- Hide  
- Fear  
- Break  

NPC Template:
- Name  
- Role  
- Personality  
- Catchphrase  
- Secret  
- Sponsor  
- Pet opinion  
- Broadcast presence  
- Quest hook  
- Reward  

---

## Key NPCs

### **Ms. Bell**
- Former crawler  
- Teacher  
- Knows the rules  
- Warns players not to reveal understanding  
- Hiding something  
- Terrified of the AI  

### **Mister Tuesday**
- Maintenance creature  
- Polite  
- Exhausted  
- Terrified  
- Knows players “aren’t broken”

## Faction Design
Factions should:
- Have sponsors  
- Have agendas  
- Have rivalries  
- Have rewards  
- Have penalties  

Faction Template:
- Name  
- Theme  
- Sponsor  
- Goals  
- Leader  
- Rivalries  
- Influence  
- Rewards  
- Penalties
