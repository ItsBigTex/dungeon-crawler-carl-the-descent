## Loot Philosophy
Loot should be:
- Useful  
- Funny  
- Dangerous  
- Marketable  
- Broadcast-friendly  

---

## Example Items

### **HEALTH-ADE™**
“When your body says ‘please stop,’ Health-Ade™ says ‘shut up!’”  
Restores HP.  
Causes hiccups for 10 minutes.

### **Lawnmower Goblin Mower**
A possessed riding mower.  
Can be tamed.  
Can be upgraded.  
Can be sponsored.

### **Shelf Mimic Snack Pack**
Heals 1 HP.  
May bite back.
