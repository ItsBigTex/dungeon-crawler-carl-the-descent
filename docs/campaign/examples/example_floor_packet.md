# Example Floor Packet — “The Carnival of Errors”
*(A sample floor demonstrating how to use the floor template)*

## 1. Floor Identity
**Floor Name:** The Carnival of Errors  
**Tagline:** “Step right up! Reality may not.”  
**Theme:** Glitched carnival / broken entertainment system  
**Primary Sponsor:** FUNZCORP™  
**Viewer Nickname:** “The Error Carnival”

---

## 2. Floor Purpose
Teaches:
- Glitches can be weaponized  
- Audience interaction is dangerous  
- Sponsor interference can escalate rapidly  

Reveals:
- The dungeon once had a full entertainment division  
- The carnival system is corrupted  
- Some attractions remember previous crawlers  

---

## 3. Districts

### 1. Midway of Malfunctions
**Purpose:** Introduce carnival hazards  
**Hazard:** Glitched games  
**Mob Type:** Prize Gremlins  
**Loot Type:** Tickets  
**Broadcast Hook:** “Win big! Lose bigger!”

### 2. The Big Top
**Purpose:** Introduce boss mechanics  
**Hazard:** Spotlight tracking  
**Mob Type:** Clown Constructs  
**Loot Type:** Performance gear  
**Broadcast Hook:** “The show must go wrong!”

### 3. Tunnel of Love (and Screaming)
**Purpose:** Introduce emotional hazards  
**Hazard:** Memory echoes  
**Mob Type:** Heart Mimics  
**Loot Type:** Emotional tokens  
**Broadcast Hook:** “Love hurts!”

---

## 4. Hazard Profile
- Spotlight malfunctions  
- Gravity flips  
- Prize machines explode  
- Emotional feedback loops  
- Audience-triggered events  

---

## 5. Signature Mobs

### Prize Gremlin
**Theme:** Carnival worker  
**Gimmick:** Throws corrupted prizes  
**Weakness:** Distracted by applause  

### Clown Construct
**Theme:** Animatronic clown  
**Gimmick:** Splits into smaller clowns  
**Weakness:** Vulnerable to silence  

---

## 6. Boss Design

### Boss Name
**The Ringmaster of Ruin**

**Sponsor:** FUNZCORP™  
**Personality:** Overly theatrical  
**Motivation:** “Put on a good show!”  

#### Phase 1
Spotlight chase.

#### Phase 2
Summons clown constructs.

#### Phase 3
Reality collapses inside the tent.

---

## 7. NPCs

### “Ticket Tim”
**Role:** Carnival guide  
**Secret:** Knows the carnival is corrupted  
**Warning:** “Don’t trust the prizes.”  

---

## 8. Loot & Economy
- Prize tickets  
- Sponsor coupons  
- Performance gear  
- Emotional tokens  

---

## 9. Chaos Tables
- Audience laughter causes damage  
- Sponsor ads interrupt combat  
- Clown duplication glitch  
- Tunnel of Love memory surge  

---

## 10. Broadcast Layer
- Announcer commentary  
- Sponsor ads  
- Viewer polls  
- Highlight triggers  

---

## 11. Map Notes
- Midway loops  
- Big Top distortions  
- Tunnel of Love water glitches
