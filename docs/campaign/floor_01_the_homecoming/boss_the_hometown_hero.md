# Boss — THE HOMETOWN HERO (Brownwood, Texas)

## Identity
A massive humanoid figure constructed from fragments of each crawler’s memories of Brownwood.

It wears:
- Harold’s Brownwood Lions colors  
- Marvin’s old work uniform  
- Philip’s favorite jacket  
- Jarod’s mascot gear  
- Mike’s backpack  
- Brad’s placeholder symbol (a blank white square)

## Personality
- Cheerful  
- Encouraging  
- Horrifying  
- Knows too much  

## Cinematic Intro
The stadium lights slam on.  
The AI announces:

“WELCOME, CONTESTANTS!  
YOU HAVE DEMONSTRATED AN UNACCEPTABLE LACK OF MARKETABLE CONFLICT!”

The Hometown Hero descends from the scoreboard, waving.

“HELLO, BROWNWOOD! I MISSED YOU!”

---

## Phase 1 — Recognition
The boss calls out each crawler:

- “HAROLD ALWAYS TRIED HIS BEST!”  
- “MARVIN NEVER FINISHED WHAT HE STARTED!”  
- “PHILIP WAS AFRAID OF DISAPPOINTING PEOPLE!”  
- “JAROD HID HIS FEAR BEHIND HUMOR!”  
- “MIKE PRETENDED EVERYTHING WAS FINE!”  
- “BRAD… UNKNOWN DATA. PLEASE UPDATE.”  

---

## Phase 2 — Distortion
The boss’s body distorts, mixing Brownwood memories incorrectly:
- The Lions colors melt  
- The mascot becomes monstrous  
- The stadium turf ripples  
- The scoreboard shows wrong dates  

---

## Phase 3 — Revelation
The boss announces one secret per crawler (placeholder text):

- “HAROLD WAS ALWAYS AFRAID OF ______.”  
- “MARVIN NEVER FORGAVE HIMSELF FOR ______.”  
- “PHILIP HID ______ FROM EVERYONE.”  
- “JAROD STILL REGRETS ______.”  
- “MIKE CAN’T LET GO OF ______.”  
- “BRAD’S SECRET IS PENDING.”  

---

## Defeat
When the boss falls:

**ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED**  
“You killed something that looked like home.”

A loot box appears.

Inside:  
A **key**.

The AI says:
“Huh.”

Long pause.

“That’s not supposed to be in Brownwood.”

Broadcast ends abruptly.
