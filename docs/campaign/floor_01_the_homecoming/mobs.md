# Floor 01 Mobs — Brownwood, Texas

## Lawnmower Goblins
Small goblin-like creatures riding possessed lawnmowers.

**Behavior:**  
- Patrol Brownwood lawns  
- Shout “YOUR GRASS IS TOO LONG!”  
- Attack with spinning blades  

**Gimmick:**  
Gain +2 attack if fighting near a yard or field.

**Weakness:**  
Stall when hit with water or cleaning products.

**Loot:**  
- Goblin Mower (vehicle)  
- Grassblade (weapon)  
- Goblin Earplugs (reduce noise hazards)

**Pet Interaction:**  
Pets can calm the mower, turning it into a mount.

---

## Shelf Mimics
Shelves pretending to hold familiar Brownwood products.

**Behavior:**  
- Lure crawlers with HEB‑style items  
- Bite when touched  
- Sometimes contain real loot  

**Weakness:**  
Alphabetizing items stuns them.

---

## Hallway Stretchers
Tall humanoids haunting Brownwood High School.

**Behavior:**  
- Stretch corridors  
- Repeat “Class is starting soon.”  
- Force long-distance fights  

**Weakness:**  
Breaking line of sight collapses the hallway.

---

## Sponsor Drones
Floating drones with Texas-themed corporate logos.

**Behavior:**  
- Record crawlers  
- Drop loot  
- Drop hazards  
- Trigger ads mid‑combat  

**Weakness:**  
Malfunction when exposed to emotional displays.
