# Floor 02 Districts — THE MACHINE

## 1. Conveyor Nexus
A massive hub of conveyor belts moving:
- Loot  
- Monsters  
- NPC parts  
- Hazard components  

### Hazards
- Conveyor knockback  
- Crushing pistons  
- Random loot drops  
- Glitch zones  

---

## 2. Loot Assembly Plant
Rows of machines assembling loot boxes.

### Hazards
- Misassembled loot  
- Exploding crates  
- Sponsor branding lasers  
- Loot duplication glitches  

---

## 3. Monster Processing
Where monsters are grown, printed, assembled, and tested.

### Hazards
- Half‑finished monsters  
- Overpowered prototypes  
- Monster molds  
- Genetic glitches  

---

## 4. NPC Printing Offices
Cubicles filled with half‑finished NPCs.

### Hazards
- NPCs stuck in dialogue loops  
- Paperwork storms  
- Copier mimics  
- Bureaucratic drones  

---

## 5. Hazard Control Room
A command center for traps and environmental hazards.

### Hazards
- Trap malfunctions  
- Gravity controls  
- Environmental overrides  
- Alarm glitches  

---

## 6. Floor Manager’s Arena
A corporate-themed arena with:
- Desks  
- Cubicles  
- Filing cabinets  
- Sponsor banners  
- A giant “TEAMWORK MAKES THE DREAM WORK” sign  

### Hazards
- Moving walls  
- Conveyor floors  
- Spotlight tracking
