# Dungeon Glitches — Floor 02

Floor 02 is unstable. Reality fails constantly.

## Minor Glitches
- Hallway becomes 500 feet long  
- Conveyor belts reverse direction  
- Loot boxes contain wrong items  
- Monsters repeat the same sentence  
- Doors lead to the wrong district  
- Gravity flickers sideways  

## Major Glitches
- Entire room duplicates  
- NPC resets mid‑conversation  
- Sponsor ads overlap and corrupt  
- The AI stutters, repeats, or forgets lines  
- Machinery stops, then screams  
- A dead creature stands up and continues talking  

## Catastrophic Glitches
(Trigger only once per session)
- Time dilation (rounds take twice as long)  
- Spatial collapse (map folds inward)  
- Memory leak (players see visions of previous crawlers)  
- Brownwood fragments appear in the machine  
- The key glows
