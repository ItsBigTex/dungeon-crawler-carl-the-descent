# Floor 02 Mobs — THE MACHINE

## Conveyor Gremlins
Small creatures that ride conveyor belts.

**Behavior:**  
- Throw parts  
- Sabotage machinery  
- Laugh constantly  

**Weakness:**  
Slip on oil spills.

---

## Loot Technicians
Humanoid workers assembling loot.

**Behavior:**  
- Attack with tools  
- Throw malfunctioning loot  
- Call for supervisor drones  

**Weakness:**  
Overheat when forced to multitask.

---

## Prototype Monsters
Half-finished creatures.

**Behavior:**  
- Unstable  
- Overpowered  
- Glitch frequently  

**Weakness:**  
Break when exposed to emotional displays.

---

## Bureaucratic Drones
Floating drones enforcing rules.

**Behavior:**  
- Issue penalties  
- Drop paperwork hazards  
- Trigger sponsor ads  

**Weakness:**  
Confused by contradictory orders.
