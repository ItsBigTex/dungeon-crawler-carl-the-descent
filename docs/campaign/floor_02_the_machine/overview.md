# Floor 02 — THE MACHINE

When the crawlers step through the exit beneath Gordon Wood Stadium, the Brownwood simulation collapses instantly. The warm Texas night sky flickers out like a dying screen, replaced by the cold hum of machinery. The dungeon reveals its true nature: a colossal industrial backroom where monsters, loot, hazards, and entire towns are manufactured.

## Floor Tagline
**“Welcome to the Dungeon’s Backstage.”**

## Theme
Industrial horror  
Cosmic bureaucracy  
Manufactured reality  
Glitches everywhere

## Purpose
This floor teaches:
- Brownwood was a set  
- The dungeon is a machine  
- Everything is manufactured  
- The AI is malfunctioning  
- The crawlers are “not broken”  
- The key is impossible  

## Districts
1. Conveyor Nexus  
2. Loot Assembly Plant  
3. Monster Processing  
4. NPC Printing Offices  
5. Hazard Control Room  
6. Floor Manager’s Arena

## Boss
**THE FLOOR MANAGER**  
A corporate monstrosity overseeing the machinery.

## Key NPC
**Mister Tuesday** — exhausted maintenance creature.

## Special Mechanic
**DUNGEON GLITCHES** — reality fails unpredictably.

## Reward
Access to Floor 3…  
Except Floor 3 is locked.
